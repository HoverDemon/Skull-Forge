### HOW TO CONVERT THIS PROJECT INTO A PK3 ###

Open the website provided in the folder, this website works offline, 100% on your device, so no extarnal data is sent to any server

Follow instructions on the website and safe the wad

You can add specific maps, or all of them, to the pk3 itself, this is used more for testing
